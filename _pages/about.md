Hi, I'm Andi. I come from South Sulawesi, Indonesia. I studied informatics at ITS Surabaya, and now I live in Singapore.

I work at MongoDB as a Senior Solutions Architect for partners in Southeast Asia. Most of my time I spend with partners and their customers. I run demos, build proofs of concept, help with architecture questions, and help partner teams learn MongoDB so they can do it themselves.

Before MongoDB, I was a solutions consultant at New Relic, and before that at Pegasystems for three years. Before I moved to Singapore, I worked in Jakarta for about fifteen years, mostly in banking IT. I was a lead backend engineer at a digital bank, writing Go on Google Cloud. Before that I worked on core banking systems, Temenos T24 and ESB integration at Anabatic, spent a year at Bank Sumitomo Mitsui, and started my career in the IT trainee programme at BRI. Along the way I used Java, Go, .NET and AS/400.

I still think like an integrator. I like to understand the whole system, not only the database.

On this site I write about what I'm working on: MongoDB, observability, and side projects that sometimes grow bigger than planned. When I'm not at the computer, I'm probably out running.
