## 01 · Pace guide

| Zone | Pace (min/km) | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| Easy / recovery | 6:05 – 6:45 | Can talk in full sentences |
| Long run | 6:10 – 6:40 | Relaxed, steady |
| Goal marathon pace (GMP) | 5:20 | Working, but in control |
| Threshold | 4:55 – 5:05 | Hard. Can hold it for about 40 min, not more |
| 5K pace | 4:35 – 4:45 | Fast, only for short reps |
| Strides | ~30 sec | Quick and smooth, not a sprint |

**Tempo and threshold are the same pace in this plan: 4:55 – 5:05.** The difference is only the format:

- *Tempo 4 km @ thresh* = one continuous 4 km at 4:55 – 5:05
- *5×1 km @ thresh* = five 1 km reps at 4:55 – 5:05, 90 sec jog between
- *5×800 m @ 5K* = the faster one, 4:35 – 4:45, 2 min jog between

If in doubt on a Tuesday: anything that says "thresh" is 5:00/km. Anything that says "5K" is 4:40/km.

## 02 · Warm-up & cool-down

### Warm-up

**Easy days (Thu / Sat)**
- The first kilometre is the warm-up. Start slow and let the pace come up on its own. No drills.

**Quality days & race (~15 min)**
- **Easy jog 10–15 min** (the 2 km WU in the plan).
- **Drills ~5 min:** leg swings (front-back & side-side), walking lunges, high knees, butt kicks, A-skips, hip circles.
- **4–6 strides** up to target pace, walk back between each. Legs should be ready to hit pace from the first rep.

*No static stretching before a run. Save it for after.*

### Cool-down

**After quality & long runs**
- **Easy jog 5–10 min** (the 2 km CD), then walk a few minutes.
- **Stretch while still warm:** calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, 20–30 sec each. Foam roll if there is one around.
- **Eat within 30–60 min:** carbs + protein, fluids, electrolytes.

*In Singapore heat you warm up faster, so the jog can be shorter. Do not skip the cool-down walk or the refuel after the 32 km run.*

## 04 · Race-day pacing · 3:45 target

- **First 5 km** → 5:25–5:28/km. Nothing under 5:20 before 10 km. Every second gained here costs two or three after 35 km.
- **5 km to 32 km** → 5:18–5:19/km. 5:20 flat gives 3:44:59 with no margin. This stretch is where the buffer comes from.
- **Halfway** → about 1:52:45
- **After 32 km** → hold. If there is something left, use it.
- **Cold day** → if it is 8°C and legs feel good, first 5 km at 5:22–5:25 is fine. Still nothing under 5:20 early.

## 05 · Notes

- **Rest days are part of the plan.** The 3 non-run days are for recovery. Optional 1–2 strength sessions (legs, core, glutes) or an easy bike or swim.
- **Fuel on long runs.** Over 90 min, take carbs every 40–45 min. On race day aim for 50–70 g per hour. Practise it in training. Nothing new on race day.
- **Heat.** Easy paces go 10–20 sec/km slower in the heat. Use electrolytes. Long runs early morning.
- **Niggles.** Swap the quality day for easy or rest. Missing one session changes nothing. Running on an injury changes everything. Never two hard days in a row.
- **Check the goal.** The Week 17 32 km (last 10 at 5:20) and the Week 18 10 km time trial (under 48) are the two tests. Shanghai in December is 10–20°C cooler than Singapore, so a 5:20 that feels hard here will feel easier there. Only if Week 17 is a real struggle, race at 5:30 (about 3:52) instead of blowing up at 32 km.

## 06 · Race-day intake

**2–3 days before**
- Carb load: 8–10 g carbs per kg bodyweight per day. Rice, noodles, bread, bananas, juice. Less fibre and fat than usual.
- Keep drinking normally, add electrolytes once a day.
- Last big meal is lunch the day before. Dinner light and early, nothing new.

**Race morning (7:00 start)**
- 4:00 breakfast, 3 hours out: 100–150 g carbs, low fibre. Rice or toast with honey or jam, a banana. Coffee if that is the normal routine.
- 500 ml water with electrolytes between waking and 6:00.
- 6:45: one gel and a few sips of water.
- Toilet before the pen. Then stop drinking.

**During the race**
- Target: 60 g carbs per hour. One gel (~25 g) every 5 km with water does it.
- Gel at km 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35. Caffeine gels at 25 and 35. Carry 8, one spare. Last one at 35, nothing after 38.
- Water: a few sips at every station, about 100–150 ml. In 8–12°C that is enough. Do not skip stations because it is cold.
- Electrolytes: one salt tab per hour (at 10, 20, 30) or use electrolyte gels. Water only with gels, not sports drink, or the stomach will complain.
- Take the gel 100 m before the station, wash it down at the station.

**If the stomach goes**
- Skip the next gel, water only for 10 min, ease pace 10 sec/km, then resume. Do not try to catch up on missed carbs.

**After**
- Within 30 min: carbs + protein, whatever is on offer. 1.5 L fluid over the next few hours with electrolytes.

**Practise it**
- Week 15 30 km and Week 17 32 km run the exact race protocol: same gel brand, same timing, same breakfast. Nothing untested on 6 December.
